Started the day early, got into flow right away. Used different AI agents, handed them screenshots, and started wiring up code directly into my app — all from the same chat.

As of writing this, I’ve burned through _almost 4 million tokens_.

- Google AI Studio: hit the 1M token limit

- [same.new](https://same.new) (premium): maxed out 2M tokens

- Claude: ~0.2M tokens  
  💸 Cost me around $30 today — mostly Claude and [same.new](https://same.new).

![](/assets/writing/vibe-coding-1.webp)

But the output? Wild.  
I knocked out _4 days worth of dev work in just 1 day_.  
From layout to boilerplate to reusable components — the pace was unreal.

**What I learned:**

- AI _drastically_ speeds up boilerplate and layout work — HTML markup, basic structure, repetitive code... done in seconds.

- BUT, it’s not plug-and-play. You still need to analyze, tweak, and adapt the output based on your app’s structure and UI needs.

- Context is king — the more clear your prompts and inputs, the better the output.

- You’ll spend less time on HTML, CSS, and wiring up basic stuff — more time on higher-level logic and polish.

- It's like having a hyper-productive intern who’s super fast but needs close guidance.

**TL;DR:** AI is here to supercharge your speed, not replace your thinking.  
It helps you code faster — _as long as you know what you’re building_ and don't blindly trust the output.

Overall: Great ROI on time and energy.  
Now I just need sleep 😅
