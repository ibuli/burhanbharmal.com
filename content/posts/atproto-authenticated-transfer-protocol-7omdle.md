The Authenticated Transfer Protocol (ATProto) is the underlying technology of Bluesky. This article describes the core components of ATProto, including records, blobs, lexicons, DIDs, handles, and AT URIs, outlining how they structure data and identify users. It also goes over the server architecture, focusing on the roles of PDSes (Personal Data Servers), relays, AppViews, labellers, and feed generators in processing and distributing data across the network.

Read the complete blog here: [https://mackuba.eu/2025/08/20/introduction-to-atproto/](https://mackuba.eu/2025/08/20/introduction-to-atproto/)
