You don’t always need JavaScript.

But sometimes… you really do.

For most of the web, HTML and CSS can carry the weight. But the moment you want interaction, personalisation, or, apparently, to “find love and be accepted by your peers” (thanks Lyra 😅), JavaScript sneaks back onto the stage.

The key isn’t avoiding JS altogether — it’s knowing when it’s essential, and when you can keep things simpler.
