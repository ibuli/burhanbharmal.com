Recently, we participated in a hackathon sponsored by Google Chrome, hosted on [Devpost](https://devpost.com) for the **Google Chrome Built-in AI Challenge**. This exciting event aimed to develop a web application or Chrome Extension utilizing one or more built-in Chrome AI APIs to interact with integrated models such as Gemini Nano.

Opting for a Chrome extension due to simplicity and time constraints, we dove headfirst into the competition, which saw a staggering 8,669 participants. This was my first-ever hackathon, and I’m thrilled to share the journey and lessons learned along the way.

### Harnessing the Power of Browser AI

Our project required us to leverage various browser AI features, including the prompt API, translator API, writer/rewriter API, language detector API, and summarizer API. These tools enabled us to build innovative features and solutions.

### The Goals Behind the Hackathon

The hackathon aimed to achieve several key goals:

- **Listening:** We were eager to receive feedback on early-stage APIs. This feedback helps Google better understand the problems developers face and ensures the design of APIs that meet real-world needs.

- **Exploring:** The challenge facilitated the discovery of promising applications for built-in AI. This exploration directly informs the roadmap and prioritization discussions, with access to exploratory APIs like the Prompt API.

- **Supporting:** Insights and interest from participants inform discussions with other browser vendors, working towards common standards for AI integration in web browsers.

For those interested in learning more, here’s an in-depth guide: [Built-in AI Guide.](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/ai/built-in)

Additionally, you can find a complete guide on how to enable built-in AI for the Google Chrome dev channel browser [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1VG8HIyz361zGduWgNG7R_R8Xkv0OOJ8b5C9QKeCjU0c/edit?pli=1&tab=t.0).

### What We Built

Our project, **SmartScout**, is a Chrome extension that leverages built-in AI APIs to enhance your browsing experience. Here's a quick rundown of what SmartScout offers:

- **Prompt API**: Provides intelligent suggestions and automates repetitive tasks.

- **Translator API**: Instantly translates web pages, making global content accessible.

- **Writer/Rewriter API**: Assists in drafting and improving written content with ease.

- **Language Detector API**: Detects the language of the web pages and customizes the user experience.

- **Summarizer API**: Quickly summarizes lengthy articles, saving you time and effort.
