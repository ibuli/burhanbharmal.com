# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web. Personal portfolio for Burhan Bharmal, based in Bengaluru.

## Users

Primary audience: founders and product teams considering product development, frontend architecture/performance, or component-system work. Readers follow firsthand engineering and product writing. Hiring teams can still access the complete resume.

## Product Purpose

Present the complete career, projects, articles, and public notes on an independently maintained personal website. Help prospective collaborators understand the offer, inspect actual work, and prepare a project enquiry; help readers discover relevant writing and continue to another article.

## Stack

User-approved: Bun, React, TanStack Start SSR, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, Oxfmt, Oxlint, Markdown, and Cloudflare Workers. Vite takes precedence over Rsbuild for the official Cloudflare integration.

## Evidence and constraints

Preserve six career roles, two education entries, three certifications, five projects, skills, and eight original articles. Work imagery must be actual project screenshots. Never invent metrics, clients, outcomes, a portrait, or missing publication dates. Articles, posts, and project narratives live in Markdown. Remove the former publishing platform’s labels, links to its hosted content, and preview notices from the public interface. External references to shared reading retain attribution.

## Brand commitments

The user explicitly chose: “cinamtic with immersive project showcases, more distinctive typography and carefuly choregraphed motion along with real project screenshots”. The opening and the project sequence serve the work. Reading surfaces remain calm.

## Deployment

Cloudflare deployment is deferred until the user refreshes their login. Local builds, testing, and deployment dry runs are authorized; do not trigger authentication or publish without their later instruction.

## Accessibility

Responsive reading and navigation, keyboard controls, visible focus, light and dark themes, reduced-motion support, and a complete professional resume with a disabled download placeholder until the intended PDF is provided.
