A few updates I’m personally excited about:

⌘K now opens a command palette from anywhere — instantly search, jump between modules, or run actions without breaking flow.

🛠 Added a small tools workshop with a JSON viewer (more utilities coming soon because I keep needing them myself 😄).

✨ Reworked the module switcher, sidebars, and transitions to feel calmer, lighter, and faster.

Tiny UX details, but they make Arkive feel way more pleasant to use every day.

Still local-first. Still built for keeping your digital life organized without adding noise.

Would love to hear what feels good / bad / missing if you try it: [https://arkive.me](https://arkive.me)
