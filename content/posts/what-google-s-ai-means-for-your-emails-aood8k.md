If you are like most Indians juggling work emails, family forwards, and endless bank alerts, your Gmail already feels like a digital diary.

Now imagine an AI going through all of this to create summaries or reports. That is what Google’s Gemini can now do.

A new update allows it to look into your Gmail, Drive, and even Google Chat. Google calls it a time-saver. But many people are worried about privacy. And for the 10 million-plus Indian firms using Google Workspace, the stakes are even higher.

Sundar Pichai, CEO of Alphabet Inc. and its subsidiary Google, says Google handles “deepest secrets responsibly.” That may be true, but with 800 million Indians online every day, the balance between ease and privacy matters more than ever. Convenience is useful, but control is essential.

**The bottomline**

Gemini’s deeper look into your emails can save time, but it may also stretch your privacy thin. Know your rights under DPDP, read the permissions, and choose wisely. Your data is valuable. Protect it.
