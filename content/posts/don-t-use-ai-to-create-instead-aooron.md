A lot of us use AI for creating.

_"Draft me an email to do XYZ…”_

_”Create a social media post for XYZ…”_

_”What should I do in this XYZ situation…”_

_”Solve this XYZ homework for me…”_

That is a dangerous way to use AI.Because AI is designed to love you. [](http://you.To)To make you feel smart. To make you feel like a genius.And to do its job politely, respectfully, and obediently.

That is dangerous because you will slowly get used to using AI for the most powerful thing you own - your mind.

You will outsource your thinking to AI.Slowly but steadily, your mind will start decaying, and you will rely more and more on AI for basic things.

It is like phone numbers.There was a time when we remembered them.Until mobile phones came along and we didn’t have to. [](http://to.Today)Today, we rarely do.So if we lose access to our phone, it is crazy that we do not remember important numbers.Because at some point, we stopped trying to remember them.

So what is the right way to use AI?

Use it not to create, but to elevate.

Do your work.Share it with AI.And ask it to guide you to make it better. So that it acts as your teacher and guide - not your helper. It shows you HOW to do the work, than to actually do the work.

Here is an example: Say you have to write an email.

1.  Write the email yourself.

2.  Paste it on your AI chat, share the objective you wish to achieve through the email and then state “Point 3 areas where this email does not achieve this objective and what I can do to fix it.”

3.  Based on the answer, fix it and paste it again.

4.  Repeat.

5.  After 2-3 iterations, you would have reached the end state. The difference is - you used AI as an independent entity to give you feedback. Not just to write that email.
