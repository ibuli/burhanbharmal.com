Import your X bookmarks in one click — pulls your saved posts straight from [x.com](http://x.com) into a dedicated collection inside the extension's bookmark module.

Also new: import your starred GitHub repositories (no sign-in needed), and bookmark exports from Pocket now import correctly with tags intact.

Plus a fix for a crash that could happen when switching notes mid-edit.
