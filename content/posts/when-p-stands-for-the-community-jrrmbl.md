Over the years, typing a single letter in the browser has turned into a shortcut to daily destinations:

- **F** → Facebook

- **L** → LinkedIn

- **Y** → YouTube

- **X** → X (formerly Twitter)

- **H** → Outlook (email)

- **G** → Google or GitHub

- **N** → Notion

And now, a new favorite has made its way into my flow: **P** → the community 🚀

Feels good when something new earns its place in muscle memory. Typing less, discovering more. 👨‍💻✨
