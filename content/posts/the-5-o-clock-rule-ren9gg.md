Have you ever thought about why a specific piece of technology gets popular? Usually, there's never a single reason, but I do have a theory that I believe is one of the primary drivers.  
I call it **The 5 O'Clock Rule**.  
With The 5 O'Clock Rule, the level of abstraction for solving a problem will bubble up until it allows the average developer to stop thinking about the problem.  
Unfortunately, it doesn't necessarily matter if the abstraction is elegant, or flexible, or free of leaks. What matters, f for the average developer that they can close their Jira ticket and go home – no later than 5 o'clock.  
Harsh, I know – but I think it's fair.  
After all, there's a reason _is-string_ package gets 28 million downloads a week.
