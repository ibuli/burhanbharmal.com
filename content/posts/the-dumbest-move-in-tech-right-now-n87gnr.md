Companies laying off developers due to AI-assisted coding are making a critical mistake. Instead of viewing AI as a cost-cutting measure, businesses should use it to empower developers and create more ambitious, higher-quality products that address the pent-up demand for specialized software. AI allows developers to have much higher productivity and reimagine products from the ground up.
