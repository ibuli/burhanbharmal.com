Moved all my newsletters out of Mail and into Arkive's RSS reader a couple of weeks back. Not going back.

Mornings used to start with archiving dozens of newsletters to find the one email I actually had to answer. That's gone. When I feel like reading the Stratechery / Platformer / whatever pile, I open Arkive. When I don't, it just sits there.

If your inbox feels like a chore, newsletters are the easiest thing to move out first.

Give it a try and make your inbox less cluttered - [https://arkive.me/rss](https://arkive.me/rss)
