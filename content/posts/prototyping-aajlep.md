Prototyping is both a mindset and a skill. It demands speed, focus, and a willingness to iterate. It’s a structured approach to fail fast, and then learn faster. Linus Torvalds’ quote, _“Talk is cheap, show me the code,”_ encapsulates this philosophy perfectly.

The next time you find yourself stuck in endless discussions or overwhelmed by a complex problem, remember: build something. Let the prototype do the talking. Only then will you truly understand the path forward.

Remember, it’s easier to explain ideas when there’s code to back it up! 👀
