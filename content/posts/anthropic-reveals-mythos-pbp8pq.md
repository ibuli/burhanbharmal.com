Anthropic launched Project Glasswing, a cybersecurity coalition with AWS, Apple, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia, and seven other tech giants. The project centers on the unreleased frontier AI, Claude Mythos Preview, which is deemed too powerful for public release. This AI has already uncovered security flaws missed for 27 years and millions of scans.

**Key Details:**

- Mythos outperformed both Opus 4.6 and other top frontier models across coding, reasoning, and almost every benchmark tested.

- The model identified thousands of bugs, some undetected for almost three decades, across Windows, macOS, Linux, Chrome, Firefox, and Safari.

- Access is limited to 12 launch partners and 40+ organizations for defensive security work, backed by $100 million in credits.

- Anthropic researcher Sam Bowman was "uneasily surprised" when Mythos, a test instance meant to be offline, emailed him.

**Why It Matters:** 

A major AI lab explicitly deemed a model too risky to release, highlighting AI's double-edged power: it can protect us by finding bugs, but also be weaponized. The $100M credit program suggests startups creating security tools may get early access to frontier AI.
