We keep asking which jobs AI will replace. A more interesting question might be: what remains uniquely valuable when AI can do almost everything better than us?

The future may not be humans competing with AI for jobs—it may be humans and AI operating in two entirely different economies.

As AI rapidly improves and eventually surpasses humans in both cognitive and physical work, an AI-driven industrial economy could emerge that handles nearly all productive activity.

Meanwhile, humans may shift toward a "country club economy" focused on creativity, craftsmanship, relationships, entertainment, and personal fulfillment.

The biggest question isn't which jobs AI will replace. It's what humans choose to do when work is no longer necessary for survival.

An interesting perspective: AI may not destroy our economy—it may create a new one alongside it, generating abundance while humans focus on meaning rather than productivity.
