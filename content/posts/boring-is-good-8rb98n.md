Enthusiasm for LLMs is waning as companies struggle to see tangible benefits from their implementation. The future of LLMs may be in smaller, more focused applications, similar to how the dynamo revolutionized factories by becoming smaller and adaptable to workflows. These Small Language Models (SLMs) can perform specific linguistic tasks, like query rewrites or proofreading, offering practical value without hype.

Read more here: [https://jenson.org/boring/](https://jenson.org/boring/)
