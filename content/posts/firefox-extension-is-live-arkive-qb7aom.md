After lots of requests from the community, I’m excited to share that the **Firefox extension is officially approved** and ready for you to try. You can grab it right here[: https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/arkive/](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/arkive/)

✨ What you can do with it:

- 🔖 **Smart Bookmarks Management** Save, tag, and organize your favorite links in just one click.

- 📝 **Rich Text Notes** Jot down ideas, thoughts, or reminders using a clean and beautiful editor.

- 📰 **Built‑in RSS Reader** Follow all your favorite blogs and sites—right from your New Tab.

- 🔄 **Account‑less Sync** Seamlessly sync and merge your data across devices using secure, built‑in P2P syncing—no sign‑ups needed!

Give it a spin and let me know what you think. I’d love your feedback! 😊
