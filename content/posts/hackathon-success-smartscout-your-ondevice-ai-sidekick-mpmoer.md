We're thrilled to announce the successful completion of our hackathon project, SmartScout! This powerful Chrome extension leverages in-browser AI to revolutionize content creation and management. With SmartScout, tasks like summarizing articles, generating posts, and translating content become effortless and lightning-fast. It's a true game-changer for bloggers, writers, and digital marketers.

Check out our submission:  
SmartScout on Devpost: [https://devpost.com/software/smartscout](https://devpost.com/software/smartscout)

We'd love to hear your thoughts! Please give it a like, leave a comment, or share your feedback over Devpost site. 🥳

PS: Currently, the extension works with Google Chrome through manual flags enablement and local installation. We are planning to release this extension on the Chrome Web Store soon.

PPS: Don't forget to check out the video recording for a quick glimpse of what it can do!
