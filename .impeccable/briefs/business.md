# Business and reading journeys

## THESIS

Make the accepted cinematic portfolio useful to founders and product teams considering paid work, and give new readers an intentional path through the writing.

## OWN-WORLD

Preserve the established Syne/Public Sans, warm neutral and charcoal themes, copper actions, original screenshots, native scrolling, and quiet reading surfaces. This is an extension of the accepted system.

## STORY

Clear offer and primary enquiry action; three concrete engagement types; the two independent-product case studies as evidence; a short local draft-preparation flow with direct email fallback. Readers get curated starting points, actual heading anchors, author context, topic-based recommendations, and RSS.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

Homepage retains the two-line name and real Arkive stage, with an explicit React product-development offer and project discussion action. Work-with-me introduces the audience, scope and route to an enquiry. Case pages identify independent work and the actual role.

## FORM

Existing visual system, code-led extension. No new visual-world selection or generated comp. Use editorial rows for services, a native accessible form, and the existing article cards. Do not invent client engagements, metrics, testimonials, capacity, prices, or reply-time guarantees. The form prepares an email draft and never claims delivery. Original content and the complete resume remain available.
