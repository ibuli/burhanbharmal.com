I'm thrilled to roll out Arkive v1.2.0 — our biggest upgrade yet! Here is what’s new in your workspace and extension:

✨ Highlights in v1.2.0:

• 🗂️ Active Tabs Manager (Bookmarks Integration):

Control your open browser tabs right inside Arkive! View tab groups, color-coded tab clusters, search open tabs in real-time, focus any tab, or save an entire window to your bookmarks in one click.

• 🎨 Brand New Look & Feel:

We’ve upgraded our design engine to Tailwind CSS v4 and Base UI primitives (@base-ui/react). Enjoy smoother animations, refined dialogs, cleaner tooltips, and a unified sidebar tree across Notes, Bookmarks, and RSS. Check out the component showcase at /design-system!

• ⚡ Lightning-Fast Module Performance:

\- Bookmarks: Rebuilt with a high-speed Zustand store and sub-millisecond search indexing across thousands of links.

\- Smart Duplicate Finder: Quickly identify and batch-clean duplicate URLs and domains in Settings.

\- Notes Editor: Zero-latency TipTap & Yjs CRDT note editing with cover image controls & reference menus.

\- RSS Reader: Upgraded sidebar tree with instant folder folding and responsive reading panes.

\- JSON Viewer Tool (/tools): Completely rewritten tree viewer with instant parsing, path copying, and node depth controls.

• 🛠️ Extension Bridge Upgrades:

Enhanced bi-directional background service worker sync between the browser runtime and Arkive web apps.

Upgrade your extension or visit [https://arkive.me](https://arkive.me) to check out the new look!
