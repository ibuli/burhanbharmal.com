# Footer artwork

## Current sketch — 22 November 2026

`public/assets/images/footer-sketch.webp` is original decorative artwork generated with OpenAI image generation, converted to WebP at quality 80 (109,140 bytes). It replaces the earlier abstract print. Broad inspiration: the quiet landscape beneath link columns in [Alfi’s Plait footer post](https://x.com/AlfiFromToasty/status/2117693388749443320), discovered through [Coolest Design’s hand-drawn collection](https://coolest.design/hand-drawn-design). The reference artwork was not copied or used as an input image.

Prompt:

> Original refined pencil-sketch background for an editorial portfolio website footer, wide landscape 16:9. Extremely delicate graphite and warm sepia ink linework on pale warm ivory paper #efeeea. A quiet imaginary garden landscape drawn in an architect's travel sketchbook: a graceful leafy tree enters from lower left, gently rolling hills and an understated modern garden studio at the far lower right, thin winding path, a few grasses. Most detail within the bottom 30 percent, faint distant contour lines at the sides. Upper 65 percent almost empty warm ivory negative space for website text. Refined hand-drawn loose contours, sparse precise hatching, lightly imperfect pencil, subtle paper texture. Very limited charcoal, muted copper and paper palette. No thick brushstrokes, no dark filled areas, no painted washes, no gradients, no text or lettering, no logos, no borders, no website interface. Flat scanned sketchbook artwork, elegant and understated, original composition.

The sketch spans the complete footer, including its bottom row. Light mode uses 42% opacity; dark mode uses 30% with an inverted colour treatment. It remains aria-hidden and non-interactive.

## Monogram and social cards — 22 November 2026

The favicon is an original path-based uppercase B with an ivory letter on charcoal and a warm copper dot. `scripts/brand-assets.ts` is its editable source and generates the platform icon sizes and ICO. The same script creates social cards using the site's Syne and Public Sans fonts and existing, attributed article/project imagery. It does not generate or alter project screenshots. All cards are 1200 × 630 JPEGs with lettering rendered as paths for reproducible builds.
